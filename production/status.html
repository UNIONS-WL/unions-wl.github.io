Shape measurement of the full UNIONS DR6 r-band footprint, run in batches of ~25 tiles on the Alliance cluster Nibi under two allocations. A batch counts as processed once it produces its final catalogue; it is published once it passes the product checks and is merged into the latest/ catalogue. This page refreshes every two hours from the scheduler and the product tree.

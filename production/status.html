UNIONS v2 weak lensing: where the analysis stands

Plate I

The UNIONS sky, and the analysis drawn on it

In green, the 4,690 deg² of northern sky where 170 million galaxy shapes pass the lensing cuts. In blue, the part that also has z-band photometry and so carries tomographic bins. The survey keeps clear of the Milky Way.

In red, the validation. The pipeline must recover known inputs from mocks, the mocks must look like the data, and the data are measured blinded. Each small star is one run of the null tests.

Log in with your CADC account first; the directory link only opens once you are logged in. Start with the README.md in its directory.
